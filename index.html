The quick answer

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is an organized collection of product references used for shopping research. A useful entry includes a source link, selected options, measurements and notes. A spreadsheet entry alone does not confirm availability, authenticity or quality.

This SuperBuy buying guide helps you organize that research. Start with a category above, then use the fit, photo and cost checks below before deciding what belongs on your shortlist.

Fit starts with measurements, not letters

A SuperBuy size guide is most useful when it starts with something you already wear. Lay that garment flat and measure it consistently. For T-shirts and hoodies, compare chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeves. For pants, record the waist, rise, thigh and inseam.

Check whether the seller reports flat widths or full circumferences. A flat chest measurement and a body chest measurement are different quantities. Keep the unit beside every number, and allow for your preferred fit rather than automatically choosing your usual letter size.

For matching sets, check the top and bottoms separately. For jerseys and fitted layers, compare the intended silhouette as well as the measurements.

Read QC photos as evidence

SuperBuy QC photos can be useful when they show the actual item being assessed. Start by checking the selected color, size label, item count and visible condition. Look for clear views of seams, closures, soles, prints and any area you specifically asked to inspect.

Separate seller photos from warehouse photos in your notes. Lighting and camera angle can change how color or proportions appear. If a critical detail is unclear, ask for clarification before relying on it.

Photos do not independently prove authenticity, fabric composition, durability or comfort. Check any available return window before arranging onward shipping.

Keep product cost and parcel cost separate

Researching SuperBuy shipping costs starts with a complete cost list. Record the product price in its original currency, then note domestic delivery, applicable service charges, payment or conversion costs, international shipping and destination import charges.

Parcel weight, dimensions, destination and service can affect an estimate. Recheck the available options when the packed parcel details are known. Consolidating items may change the quote; compare the resulting totals rather than assuming the combined parcel is cheaper.

A product screenshot is not a delivered-cost quote. Use current seller and service information for your own destination.

Straight answers

Before you continue.

Practical answers for researching a spreadsheet, reading listings and understanding where these links lead.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is an organized collection of product references used for shopping research. A useful entry includes a source link, selected options, measurements and notes. A spreadsheet entry alone does not confirm availability, authenticity or quality.

How do I use this page?

Choose a category near the top, browse the destination listings and record the exact item and selected options. Then compare measurements, review available product information and estimate the total cost. Category links open the specified cafans.com categories; other navigation links open the cafans.com homepage.

Are these links direct SuperBuy checkout links?

No. This page links to cafans.com for product discovery. If you intend to use a buying agent, confirm that the original seller link is supported and review that agent's current terms before ordering.

Do QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible condition, selected options and requested measurements. They do not independently establish authenticity, material composition, long-term durability or comfort.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Measure a well-fitting garment and compare the same points with the listing's size chart. Check whether dimensions are flat measurements or full circumferences, use the same units and allow for your preferred fit.

Does the product price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check whether domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion and import charges are included. The amount depends on the seller, service, parcel and destination.

Is this an official SuperBuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide directing visitors to cafans.com. It has no official affiliation with SuperBuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Hermès or other brands mentioned. Reference images do not indicate current stock or an authentication guarantee.
